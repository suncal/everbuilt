07 — FAQ

The things everyone asks

How can hosting possibly be $0/month?
We build your site on modern edge infrastructure (Cloudflare Pages and GitHub) whose free tiers include unlimited bandwidth and global delivery. No servers, no databases to babysit, nothing that accrues a bill. It's not a discount or a trial — it's an architecture choice. Our national nonprofit client has paid $0 in hosting since launch day.
Do I really own everything?
Yes — the code repository, the content, the domain, the admin access. If we disappeared tomorrow, your site would keep running and any developer could pick it up. We think "fire us anytime and lose nothing" is the only honest way to sell websites.
How is "days, not months" possible without cutting corners?
Two reasons: we've productized the hard parts (media pipelines, form wiring, DNS cutovers — battle-tested on a national launch), and we don't run the agency meeting-industrial-complex. No discovery workshops, no three rounds of wireframes. You see a finished remake first, then we build the rest at the same standard.
Can I update the site myself without paying you?
Yes. Signature builds include an admin panel for photos, pages, and content — our nonprofit client updates events through a spreadsheet and the site refreshes within minutes. No code, no tickets, no hourly billing.
What happens to my email and domain during launch?
Nothing — and we put that in writing. Your domain stays at your current registrar; we change only the web records and never touch email. Our cutover runbook launched a national site with zero minutes of downtime.
Why are you so much cheaper than agencies — and pricier than Fiverr?
Agencies carry account managers, discovery workshops, and office overhead into every quote — that's the $35–60K. Marketplace gigs hand you a template and disappear — that's the $500. We're a lean studio with a productized build system proven on a national launch: you get agency-grade output without agency overhead, and unlike the cheap option, you own a site that's architected to run free forever.
What if I don't like the free remake?
Then you tell us, and that's the end of it — no follow-up sequence, no pressure calls. You keep the design. The remake is our pitch; if it doesn't win you over on sight, we haven't earned the project.
What if I need a web app or mobile app, not a website?
That's our Platform tier. We build web apps, SaaS MVPs, and mobile apps with the same philosophy: fixed-scope quotes, lean builds, fast shipping, and you own the codebase outright.
