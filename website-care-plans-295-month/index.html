Website Care Plans $295/Month

Yes, you can add ongoing support to your Everbuilt Studio site for $295 a month. After the free 90‑day care period you choose a plan and we keep your site updated, backed up, and running smoothly.

How it works for a newly launched site

  1. Your site launches with a Launch package ($2,950) or higher.
  2. We give you 90 days of free care—updates, minor tweaks, and monitoring.
  3. When the free period ends, you select the $295/month care plan.
  4. Each month we handle CMS updates, security patches, and any small changes you request.
  5. You can cancel anytime; there’s no contract lock‑in.

What you get for $295/month

  • Ongoing site maintenance and updates
  • Security monitoring and backups
  • Minor content edits (text, images, links)
  • Email and form integration checks
  • Priority response within one business day

The plan is a one‑time monthly fee. No hidden charges, no hosting fees beyond the $0/month Cloudflare Pages hosting that’s already included with every site.

FAQ

What if I need a larger change after the first month? Minor edits are covered. For major redesigns you would purchase a new project or upgrade to the $595/month plan.

Do I still own the site outright? Yes. All code, content, and the domain remain yours. The care plan only adds service, not ownership changes.

Can I stop the care plan at any time? Yes. Cancel any month; you keep full control of the site and its files.

Is the $295 price the same for all site types? The $295/month plan applies to any site built with Everbuilt Studio, whether it’s a simple 5‑page launch or a larger Signature project.
