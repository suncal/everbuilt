Nonprofit Website Design Package

Yes, this is a full‑service nonprofit website design package from Everbuilt Studio. You get a custom site you own outright, hosted for $0/month, and 90 days of free care after launch.

How it works for a nonprofit looking for a quick, professional site

  1. Request the free homepage remake to see the design style – no call, no payment details.
  2. Choose the Launch package ($2,950) for up to 5 pages, custom design, contact forms and basic SEO.
  3. Pay a 50 % deposit; we build the site in about a week.
  4. Review the live site, approve the final version, and the balance is due at launch.
  5. Enjoy 90 days of free care; after that you can add a monthly care plan if you wish.

What you get and the costs

  • Launch package – $2,950 total, includes up to 5 pages, custom design, contact forms, SEO basics, and a week‑long turnaround.
  • Ownership – you receive all code, content, and the domain; the site is yours to keep or modify.
  • Hosting – $0/month on Cloudflare Pages and GitHub, no hidden fees.
  • Post‑launch support – 90 days free care, then optional plans at $295/month or $595/month, cancel anytime.
