Subscribe to a $295 Website Care Plan

After your site’s free 90‑day care ends, you can keep it updated and backed up for $295 a month. The plan covers routine maintenance, security patches, and a few small content edits each month.

How the plan works

  1. Launch your site with a Launch ($2,950), Signature ($7,500), or Platform ($15K) package.
  2. Receive 90 days of free care that includes updates, minor fixes, and monitoring.
  3. When the free period ends, the $295/month plan begins automatically unless you cancel.
  4. Everbuilt Studio handles regular updates, security monitoring, backups, and up to a few small content changes each month.
  5. You may cancel at any time; there is no long‑term contract.

What’s included

  • Monthly fee: $295 (upgrade to $595 for premium care).
  • Maintenance: routine code updates and security patches.
  • Backups: regular site backups stored on Cloudflare Pages and GitHub.
  • Content edits: a few small changes per month, such as text tweaks or image swaps.
  • Ownership: you keep full ownership of the code, content, and domain.
  • Hosting: $0/month on Cloudflare Pages and GitHub.

FAQ

What happens after the 90‑day free care period? You can continue with the $295/month plan or upgrade to $595/month. The service is optional and can be stopped at any time.

Can I start the care plan before the free period ends? Yes. You may subscribe at any point; the first billed month starts after the free period.

What if I need larger changes or new features? Major redesigns or new functionality are billed separately, using the same one‑time fees for Launch, Signature, or Platform projects.
